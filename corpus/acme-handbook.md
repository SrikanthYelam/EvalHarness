# Acme Employee Handbook

## Time Off

### Vacation
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 vacation days per month, for 18 days per year. Unused days up to 5 carry over into the next calendar year; anything above that expires on January 31.

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. The company reimburses up to $50 per month for home internet. Equipment such as monitors and keyboards can be requested through the IT portal, with a one-time budget of $400.

## Expenses

Submit expense reports within 30 days of purchase using the finance portal. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Travel must be booked through the approved travel agency; economy class is required for flights under six hours.
